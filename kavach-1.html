A crop claim shouldn't take forty days
to settle in four minutes.

Kavach reads field damage the way an assessor would, checks it against independent weather data, and lets a smart contract pay — or block — the claim the moment the evidence is verified. No adjuster visit. No paper trail. No waiting for the next monsoon.
